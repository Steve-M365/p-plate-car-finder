Victorian P-plate vehicle rules (implemented by this app)

{{ rules_summary }}

Thresholds in use

  • Power-to-mass limit: 130 kW per tonne (tare mass).
  • Ratios of roughly 120–130 kW/t (kerb-weight estimate) are reported as verify because the official figure uses lighter tare mass.
  • Applies to both P1 and P2 drivers of any age in Victoria.
  • No blanket turbo / V8 / rotary ban in Victoria since 29 October 2019.
  • The old absolute power caps (~125/149 kW) are obsolete; this app ships with the absolute cap disabled.

Exemptions

  • Learner permit holders, full licence holders and overseas licence holders are not subject to the prohibited-vehicle rule.
  • A supervising fully-licensed driver may allow a prohibited vehicle.
  • Automatic work-related exemption (employer requirement, self-employed with ABN, or learning a heavy vehicle).
  • Undue-hardship exemption on application to VicRoads.
