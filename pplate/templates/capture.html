Save a listing from any site

This is the reliable, terms-friendly way to get real listings from carsales, Gumtree, Facebook Marketplace, Autotrader or any dealer site. You capture a page you are already viewing — the app never crawls a site or bypasses any access control.

  1. Drag the button below to your browser's bookmarks bar (once).
  2. Open a car listing, then click the bookmarklet.
  3. It opens a new tab that parses the page, saves the car, and tags P-plate compliance.
